## 1.0.0

First release.

- Month, fit-week, three-day, day and schedule views of UTC events.
- Overlapping events laid out side by side; events crossing midnight continue
  on the next day.
- Pinch zoom of the hourly views, with accessible zoom controls.
- Paging between periods, with a navigation helper and support for animated
  transitions and previews.
- Taps on events, days and empty time; a provisional `draft` slot.
- Moving events by long-press and drag, across days and periods.
- Highlighting an event, and hints for events scrolled out of sight.
- App-supplied event colors, leading indicators, tooltips and schedule rows.
